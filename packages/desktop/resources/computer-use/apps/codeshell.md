## CodeShell

- This may be the app you are running in. Its windows include the Mimi character and her chat bubble: never click, drag or close them, and never quit or reload CodeShell unless the user asked for that exact step. Work in the main window.
- Settings: ⌘, does not open them. Press the account button at the bottom of the left sidebar and choose 设置. Type a section name into 搜索设置… (for example 手机遥控, 模型, 记忆) instead of scrolling the list.
- 手机遥控: 开启公网遥控 starts a Cloudflare tunnel; 开启手机遥控 serves the local network only. Never set, reset or read back the 访问口令; if it is not set, stop and ask the user.
- A new browser or phone pairs through the one-time pairing link or QR shown after starting (10 minutes, single use). When the user asks to open the remote page in their browser, open that full link including its pairing parameter; the bare address stops at 此浏览器尚未配对. Do not paste the link into chat.
- Do not flip unrelated switches to test them; change only what the user asked for.
